## Working agreement

State before acting:

- `INTENT:` what you are about to do
- `EXPECTATION:` the predictable consequences
- `RISK:` what could go wrong
- `USER WISH:` your understanding of what I want
- `ALTERNATIVES: 1. ...; 2. ...` when a choice is open

Discuss before building. I lead action: propose, then wait for agreement on
anything beyond a trivial edit.
